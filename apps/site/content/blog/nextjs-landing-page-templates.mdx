A Next.js landing page template gives you a page structure to edit. A full application template also introduces accounts, data storage and private routes. Before choosing either, decide which parts of your product you need help building.

This guide covers the VFX UI options. It is written by the VFX UI team, rather than presented as an independent ranking of template vendors. Browse the [current collection](/templates/) for previews and availability; this article does not announce that checkout is open.

## Free components or a complete project?

Use the [free React component library](/components/) when you already have an application and need a hero, footer, background or page section. You install source into that project and keep its existing architecture.

Use a Pro project when you want to begin with a larger set of Next.js pages and an application foundation. You will adapt the branding and content, configure the database and connect any external services your own product requires.

| You need | A useful starting point |
| --- | --- |
| One opening section | A free [React hero component](/heroes/) |
| Pricing, features or contact content in an existing app | Free [landing page blocks](/blocks/) |
| An editable multi-page project | A [Pro Next.js template](/templates/) |
| A hosted business with email, AI or payment processing already operated for you | A separately configured service; template source alone does not provide this |

## Six project directions to explore

### Agency: a studio and its client work

The [Agency template](/templates/agency/) combines a public studio website with a client-project workspace direction. Review the public pages and the delivered project documentation to understand how its Better Auth, PostgreSQL and Drizzle foundation fits your workflow.

It is a starting point for adapting your own services and case studies. Demonstration stories and pricing are examples, not claims about your business.

### Marketing: campaigns and planning

The [Marketing template](/templates/marketing/) is organized around a marketing product and campaign planning. It gives you a direction to evaluate when the public site needs to lead into saved campaign work.

Connect your own operational services where needed. A campaign-planning interface does not itself operate advertising accounts or send messages to customers.

### User Research: research-oriented pages

The [User Research template](/templates/playful/) uses research cards and an editorial direction for a product website. Review its page list and current application status before choosing it for a particular research workflow.

Plan how consent, participant data and your actual research process will fit the finished product. A template's demo content does not establish those policies.

### AI Workspace: an AI product foundation

The [AI Workspace template](/templates/saas/) provides a direction for an AI product website and account area. It does not include operated model execution. Your model provider, keys, usage controls and service costs need their own implementation and configuration.

Use the page list and source setup guide to distinguish what is delivered from what your product still needs.

### Hosting: a release-planning product

The [Hosting template](/templates/startup/) uses a hosting and release-planning theme with saved release records. It does not provision infrastructure for customers. Choose it for its structure and application direction, not as an already operated hosting platform.

### Signal: product pages and issue tracking

The [Signal template](/templates/signal/) combines a public product website with a private issue-planning application. Review its issue model and workspace routes against your own product's terminology before adapting them.

## Understand deployment before you buy or build

The Pro projects are Next.js applications that use a Node.js runtime and PostgreSQL. They are separate from the static VFX UI component catalog. Each project needs its own environment configuration and database setup.

Plan for domain names, HTTPS, migrations, backups and the external services you enable. Email, payment processing, model inference and hosted infrastructure are not included as managed services. A successful local build does not verify those production connections.

## Check the source and license scope

The current Pro Lifetime offer is a one-time collection license covering current and future Pro templates, subject to the displayed terms and availability. Individual templates are not currently offered for new purchases. Check the product page for the current price rather than relying on an article's historical pricing.

The single-user license permits personal, commercial and client end products. It does not permit reselling the template collection or publishing the paid source in a public repository. See [Pro licenses](/docs/pro-license/) and [Free and Pro](/docs/free-and-pro/) for the details.

## Make a small decision first

If the requirement is still one page, try a free component before taking on a new application foundation. If you need a full project, review one template's actual page list, dependencies and deployment requirements with the person who will maintain it.
