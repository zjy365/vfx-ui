---
title: Motion & accessibility
description: Keep the content useful with reduced motion, touch, keyboard and unavailable GPU support.
---

## Start with content

Effects should support your message. Keep headings, links and calls to action in semantic HTML. A visual effect must not be the only way to understand an action or state.

## Reduced motion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Components may keep static artwork while removing pointer movement and continuous animation. Test individual component behavior before shipping your page.

## Touch and keyboard

Pointer effects are optional enhancements. Provide native links and buttons, visible focus styles, readable contrast and useful touch targets. Do not make hover the only way to reach content.

## GPU fallback

Test the target browsers and devices. A browser without WebGPU should still display your content and a static visual fallback. Avoid placing essential text inside a GPU canvas.
