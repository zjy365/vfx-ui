The solo license permits one user to modify the templates and create unlimited personal, commercial and client end products. You may deliver a finished client website with the source needed to maintain that website.

You may not resell or redistribute the template collection, offer its source as a competing template or component product, share a download account, or publish the paid template source in a public repository. Original third-party open source dependencies retain their own licenses.

The current offer is Pro Lifetime: $199 USD once for all current and future Pro templates, with lifetime use and updates. Individual templates are not currently offered for new purchases. Any existing single-template license retains its original scope and updates. Separately sold content-creation skills and hosted services are excluded.

This page describes the intended product terms. The terms shown at a live checkout govern the purchase. Team licensing is not part of the initial offer.

The free component library has its own [MIT license guide](/docs/license/).
