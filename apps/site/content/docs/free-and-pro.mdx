## Free library

The published free components remain open source under MIT. You may use and modify them in commercial products. Keep the applicable copyright and license notices when redistributing source.

## Pro Collection 01

Agency, Marketing, User Research, AI Workspace, Hosting and Signal are complete Next.js applications built with VFX UI.

The current offer is **Pro Lifetime: $199 USD once** for all current and future Pro templates. It includes lifetime use and updates, and allows one user to create unlimited end products, including client websites. Individual templates are not currently offered for new purchases. Separately sold content-creation skills and hosted services are excluded.

## What a template includes

Editable Next.js source, responsive layouts, Better Auth accounts, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, a persistent workspace and setup instructions. Each project runs on your own server and database. External AI, email, analytics, payment and deployment providers require your own configuration; hosted services are not included.

## Delivery

Purchasers sign in with an email code or an available social provider to view their purchases and available versioned source downloads. Source archives are private. Installed templates do not call a license server at runtime.

Availability is shown on the product page. Checkout remains disabled until the delivery artifact and payment configuration are ready.
