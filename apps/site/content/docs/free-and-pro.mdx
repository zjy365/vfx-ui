## Free library

The published free components remain open source under MIT. You may use and modify them in commercial products. Keep the applicable copyright and license notices when redistributing source.

## Pro Collection 01

One collection includes Agency, Marketing, Playful, SaaS and Startup: complete responsive landing page projects built with VFX UI.

The proposed solo price is **$59 once**. The license allows one user to create unlimited end products, including client websites. It includes 12 months of updates to this fixed collection, with permanent use of versions already delivered. New collections are separate products.

## What a template includes

Editable project source, page content and configuration, responsive layouts, setup instructions and component usage. Any demonstrated account, email, analytics or payment interface needs its own service configuration; templates do not include a hosted SaaS backend.

## Delivery

When the collection is released, purchasers sign in to view their purchase and download a versioned source archive. Source archives are private. Installed templates do not call a license server at runtime.

Availability is shown on the product page. Checkout remains disabled until the delivery artifact and payment configuration are ready.
