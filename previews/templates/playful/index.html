A few useful answers

Before you begin.

Do I need to bring a skill?+

Curiosity is a good beginning. A welcoming gathering makes room for listening, asking questions and trying something for the first time.

Where can I find event details?+

A real invitation should give a location, time, cost and accessibility information. Stories on this concept site are fictional and do not describe bookable events.

Does the invitation form sign me up?+

It prepares a message in your email app. It does not add you to a mailing list or create a membership. Send the email to begin a conversation.
