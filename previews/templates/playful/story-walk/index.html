Concept story / Outside

A walk with no shortcut.

A chance to notice what the ordinary route leaves out.

Take the longer turn past a familiar street. A neighbourhood changes when there is enough time to notice the trees, the shop windows and the places people pause.

A walk does not need to be an achievement. It can be a gentle reason to meet, exchange a few observations and return with a different sense of an ordinary place.

This concept story describes no scheduled event. Replace it with a real route, meeting point, duration and accessibility notes before inviting people along.
