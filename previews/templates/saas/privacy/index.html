Your privacy.

This static concept website does not run analytics, advertising cookies, accounts or a hosted form inbox.

Contact forms prepare a mailto draft locally in your browser. Information is sent only when you choose to send that email through your email provider. The website does not store form submissions.

Fonts and interface assets are served with the website. Your hosting provider may process standard connection logs, such as your IP address, for delivery and security. Replace this notice with the actual policy of your operator and host.

If you add analytics, payments, authentication or another form service, document the actual data collection, retention and contact rights before launch. Add consent controls where the added services require them.
