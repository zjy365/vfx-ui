Field note / September 29, 2026

An invitation before a launch.

An early list can start a conversation before it becomes a broadcast channel.

Before asking someone to join a list, explain what the conversation is for. A specific question gives an early invitation a useful shape.

A small group of thoughtful replies can be more valuable than a larger number with no shared context. Leave room for people to tell you what is missing from your assumptions.

This template uses an email draft rather than a subscription backend. Connect a real consent and mailing-list service only when you can explain what will be sent and how people can leave.
