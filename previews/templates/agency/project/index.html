Concept case study / Furniture

Forma. A shape for a new idea.

A fictional identity and digital concept, created to show how this template handles a complete project narrative.

03 / Make it useful

Test the reading order and navigation on the smallest screen first.

The question was simple: how can modular furniture feel less like a system and more like a personal choice? This concept begins with a single circle, split and recombined across the identity.

The visual language balances generous whitespace with a compact typographic hierarchy. Product information gets the room it needs; expressive moments sit at the edges rather than competing with the work.

Deliverables shown here are a concept scope: positioning, identity principles, typography, art direction and a responsive digital system. No real client relationship or commercial results are claimed.
