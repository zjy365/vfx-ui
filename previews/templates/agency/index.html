A few useful answers

Before you begin.

What does a first conversation cover?+

Start with your audience, the change you want to make, and the decisions your existing identity or website makes difficult. A good scope grows from the question.

Can the studio work on an existing identity?+

A project can begin with what is already useful. The discovery process should decide whether a focused extension or a new visual language will serve the work.

What happens after the website is ready?+

Plan a clear handover: content editing, deployment access, documented components and an agreed support scope. Replace these example service details with your actual process.
