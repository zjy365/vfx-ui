# vfx-ui

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
An open-source React visual-effects library focused on Hero and Footer sections, with a website for exploring, customizing, and installing its components.

## Users
Developers and designers integrating expressive effects into their own websites (inferred from the existing library and documentation).

## Capabilities and Constraints
React 18, TypeScript, Vite; WebGPU effects through vgpu and a WebGL globe through cobe. MIT licensed. Keep existing Hero exports and routes working. At the user’s request, remove LiveChart, WebGlobe and EnergyOrb from the source, routes and install catalog. Registry installation and agent-readable documentation must reflect the current catalog. Demonstration content must remain replaceable. Interactive DOM components must work without WebGPU.

## Brand Commitments
Keep vfx-ui and the existing Hero effects and their visual character. The user explicitly requests a striking, creative result and meaningful mouse interaction, and delegates implementation decisions. Apply the supplied Anshu design methodology: a specific direction, independent screenshot criticism, concise copy and a final subtraction pass.

## Product Principles
- Demonstrate the real component, not a marketing simulation.
- Effects respond to people and accept their content.
- One component is one catalog entry; presets belong inside it.
- Preserve keyboard access, touch usability and reduced-motion preferences.
