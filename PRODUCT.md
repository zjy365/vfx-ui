# vfx-ui

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
An open-source React visual-effects library: Hero and Footer sections, GPU backgrounds, focused DOM interactions, and complete installable page Blocks (navigation, showcase, features, pricing, FAQ, CTA and more) for building product and marketing sites — with a website for exploring, composing, and installing everything.

## Users
Developers and designers integrating expressive effects and complete page sections into their own websites (inferred from the existing library and documentation).

## Capabilities and Constraints
React 18, TypeScript, Vite; WebGPU effects through vgpu and a WebGL globe through cobe. MIT licensed. Keep existing Hero exports and routes working. At the user’s request, remove LiveChart, WebGlobe and EnergyOrb from the source, routes and install catalog. Registry installation and agent-readable documentation must reflect the current catalog. Demonstration content must remain replaceable. Interactive DOM components must work without WebGPU. Blocks are pure DOM/CSS (no WebGPU), installable individually, and compose into complete example pages (product launch, design studio) whose copy, links and prices are fictional demo content marked on screen.

## Brand Commitments
Keep vfx-ui and the existing Hero effects and their visual character. The user explicitly requests a striking, creative result and meaningful mouse interaction, and delegates implementation decisions. Apply the supplied Anshu design methodology: a specific direction, independent screenshot criticism, concise copy and a final subtraction pass.

## Product Principles
- Demonstrate the real component, not a marketing simulation.
- Effects respond to people and accept their content.
- One component is one catalog entry; presets belong inside it.
- Preserve keyboard access, touch usability and reduced-motion preferences.
- Blocks ship replaceable everything: no block should require editing its source to be used.
- Demo testimonials, prices and companies are invented and visibly labeled; never present them as real social proof.
